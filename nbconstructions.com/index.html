WHY N.B. CONSTRUCTIONS?

NBCPL is a nationally recognized construction company, a unit of Nirman Bharti Group based in Gurgaon, Haryana working as a one-stop solution for all construction-related services to all sizes of organizations ranging from institutional, industrial, commercial, and individual clients nationwide. We are a constantly growing and evolving team of experts, with a staff strength of over 150 skilled professionals and around 3000 manpower.

N.B. CONSTRUCTIONS PVT LTD

BUILDERS TO NATIONS
 

N.B. Constructions Pvt Ltd has over the past two decades been transforming cityscapes and landscapes with structures of immense size and grandeur. The company’s capabilities span the entire gamut of construction-plumbing, fire, landscaping, air conditioning, solar panels installation, green walls, external elevation facade, elevation glazing, and its services extend to all core sectors.

Mission

To be the pre-eminent provider of construction services by consistently improving the quality of our products, to add value for clients through innovation, foresight, integrity & aggressive performance, and to serve with character & purpose that brings honor to God.

Vision

To perform for our clients the highest level of quality construction services at a fair & most competitive prices according to the current market scenarios. To empower the people associated with NB and help in overall development of their lives

Aim

Our aim as a team, we wish to build sustainable projects for both government and private project. Also, keep on increasing the professionalism instilled in us by our founder and also to become India's Top Level Contractor in the coming years by successfully completing bigger projects.

Quality

Quality is never an accident. It is always the result of intelligent effort N.B constructions entire team works on the above cited ideology. We constantly work in the direction of improving the quality with the implementation of new technologies being tested on site. We have already applied for ISO Certification as a step towards achieving International quality standards.

Values

To maintain the highest level of professionalism, integrity, honesty and fairness with our clients, sub-contractors, suppliers & associates.
